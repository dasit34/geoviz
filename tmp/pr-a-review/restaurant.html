Section 04 · Diagnosis

Top 3 issues

What's Holding You Back

  1. #1

    Your website isn't showing up anywhere online

    High Impact
    Problem
    The site returned no content in any search engine fetch; Google, Bing, and AI tools cannot find or read it.
    Why it hurts visibility
    When a local customer asks ChatGPT or Google "where should I eat tonight," your restaurant doesn't exist in the answer — every reservation goes to a competitor instead.
  2. #2

    AI tools have no way to understand what your business is

    High Impact
    Problem
    No business name, location, cuisine, phone number, or hours were confirmed on any readable version of the homepage.
    Why it hurts visibility
    Even if the site becomes accessible, AI tools will skip over it because they can't tell a user what you serve, where you are, or whether you're open — the three questions every diner asks.
  3. #3

    There's no content for customers (or AI) to trust

    Critical
    Problem
    No menu descriptions, no FAQ, no reviews, and no "about us" content were found anywhere the site can be read.
    Why it hurts visibility
    Restaurants with visible menus, real pricing, and customer reviews consistently beat restaurants with blank or inaccessible pages — the trust gap translates directly into empty tables.

These fixes directly map to the issues above, but this section focuses only on action.

Section 05 · Action plan

Top 3 fixes

What To Fix First

  1. #1

    Make the website publicly accessible and indexed

    Quick WinDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Quick win
    Fix
    Confirm the site is live with no access blocks, submit it to Google Search Console, and create a robots.txt that explicitly allows search engines in with a sitemap reference.
    Expected impact
    The site starts appearing in Google search results and AI tools gain their first view of the business.
  2. #2

    Add your business identity clearly to every page

    High ImpactDifficulty: EasyGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix
    Add your restaurant name, city, cuisine type, phone number, and hours to the top and footer of every page — and embed a machine-readable identity block (the kind AI tools parse automatically) so the details are impossible to miss.
    Expected impact
    AI tools can now answer "what is this place and where is it?" — the minimum required to appear in local recommendation results.
  3. #3

    Publish real content: menu, FAQ, and trust signals

    Trust SignalDifficulty: ModerateGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Trust boost
    Fix
    Add a live menu with real dish names and price ranges, a short FAQ ("Do you take reservations? Do you have a private dining room?"), and a visible link to your Google reviews page.
    Expected impact
    Customers and AI tools can now evaluate your restaurant on its actual merits — turning a blank page into a page that earns bookings.
