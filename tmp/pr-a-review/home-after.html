AI search is changing how businesses get discovered.

Customers are increasingly receiving synthesized answers instead of scrolling through lists of links. Visibility now depends on whether AI systems can correctly interpret, trust, and retrieve your business information.

§01What AI sees

What AI systems actually see.

Before AI can recommend you, it has to read you. Five interpretive dimensions determine whether AI systems understand, trust, and recommend your business.

Entity clarity

Whether AI can resolve one business, one identity from name, location, and structured details.

Crawlability

robots.txt, sitemap, canonical, meta-robots — whether AI systems can reach your pages.

Trust signals

Reviews, citations, and verifications tied to the business entity — not scattered.

Retrieval readiness

Structured content AI can quote when a customer asks for a recommendation.

Recommendation confidence

Whether AI has enough signal to cite you when it assembles an answer.

§06Questions

Questions, answered.

  • What is AI visibility?+

    Whether AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity can find, understand, and recommend your business when a customer asks them for help. It’s separate from where you rank on Google.

  • Why does this matter for my business?+

    More people now ask AI for a recommendation before they ever open a search engine. If AI can’t read your business clearly, it confidently recommends a competitor instead of you.

  • Is this just SEO?+

    No. SEO is about ranking in Google’s search results. This is about whether AI systems can understand and trust your business enough to recommend it. They’re related, but they’re not the same checks.

  • What makes GeoViz different from other AI search tools?+

    Most tools query one AI system. GeoViz analyzes your business across four — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity — and reports where they agree, where they diverge, and which sources they cite about you. The result is a multi-model read, not a single-model guess.

  • Can you fix the issues for me?+

    Yes. The audit shows you exactly what’s wrong. The optional Foundation Fix is a scoped engagement where we implement those fixes for you and re-check the result.

  • How long does it take?+

    Most audits are delivered by email within minutes. Every report is reviewed by a human before it’s sent, which can occasionally add a short wait during busy periods.
