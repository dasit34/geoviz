Section 04 · Diagnosis

Top 3 issues

What's Holding You Back

  1. #1

    AI Tools Can't Confirm Your Basic Business Identity

    Trust Signal
    Problem
    Your website doesn't include a structured "business card" that AI tools can read — no confirmed firm name + address + phone + service area in a format they recognize as authoritative.
    Why it hurts visibility
    When someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI "who's a good personal injury lawyer near me in California," AI tools skip firms they can't verify and recommend competitors whose details they can read with confidence.
  2. #2

    Your Site Isn't on the AI Tools' Radar

    Quick Win
    Problem
    There's no confirmed AI-bot welcome file (robots.txt with clear open access), no XML sitemap referenced, and no llms.txt guide file, so tools like Perplexity and Claude have no clear instructions on how to read your site.
    Why it hurts visibility
    Tools that can't efficiently navigate your site default to whatever competitor they've already indexed fully — that's a call you never receive.
  3. #3

    Your Reputation Claims Don't Have Backup Proof on the Homepage

    Trust Signal
    Problem
    The homepage mentions "Top 100 member" and "award winning," but no review count, no star rating, and no third-party badge (like BBB or Google reviews) are visible where a new visitor — or an AI tool — would see them first.
    Why it hurts visibility
    An AI tool weighing whether to recommend your firm needs verifiable proof, not just internal claims; without it, a competitor with a visible "4.9 stars — 1,200 reviews" line wins the citation.

These fixes directly map to the issues above, but this section focuses only on action.

Section 05 · Action plan

Top 3 fixes

What To Fix First

  1. #1

    Install a Digital Business Card That AI Tools Can Read

    High ImpactDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix
    Add a LegalService/LocalBusiness code block to your homepage that lists your firm name, phone number, address, states served, and practice areas in a format AI tools read natively.
    Expected impact
    AI tools can now confirm who you are, what you do, and where you serve — making them far more likely to include you in a "personal injury lawyer near me" recommendation.
  2. #2

    Open the Door for AI Search Tools

    Quick WinDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Quick win
    Fix
    Configure your robots.txt to explicitly welcome citation bots (ClaudeBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot), add an XML sitemap reference, and create a simple llms.txt guide file pointing to your practice-area pages and FAQ.
    Expected impact
    Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overview all have a clear path into your site, which means your content becomes available when customers ask AI tools for referrals.
  3. #3

    Add One Line of Verifiable Proof to Your Homepage

    Trust SignalDifficulty: EasyGeoViz Profile Setup: NoEstimated impact: Trust boost
    Fix
    Add your Google review count and star rating, or a BBB badge, directly to your homepage — visible above the fold so any visitor or AI tool sees it within seconds of landing on the page.
    Expected impact
    A single line like "4.8 stars — 900+ Google reviews" transforms vague award claims into proof a tool can trust and cite.
