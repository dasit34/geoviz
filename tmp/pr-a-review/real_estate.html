Section 04 · Diagnosis

Top 3 issues

What's Holding You Back

  1. #1

    AI tools can't read your business details directly

    High Impact
    Problem
    There is no machine-readable identity on the site: no structured business card that tells AI tools your firm name, phone number, office addresses, and specialty in one place.
    Why it hurts visibility
    When someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI "who are the top commercial real estate recruiters in Boston?", AI tools skip firms they can't quickly verify — and yours is one of them.
  2. #2

    No site map means AI tools may miss your best pages

    Trust Signal
    Problem
    No sitemap.xml was found, and the robots.txt returned no usable instructions; AI citation tools have no guide to your team, process, or about pages.
    Why it hurts visibility
    Your strongest credibility content — years in business, client quotes, office locations — sits on pages that AI tools may never find, so those signals never reach a prospect's AI-generated answer.
  3. #3

    No verifiable proof-of-quality on the homepage

    Trust Signal
    Problem
    The homepage carries no star rating, no review count, no industry association badge, and no license or certification number.
    Why it hurts visibility
    When a hiring manager uses an AI tool to vet recruiting firms before making a call, the firms that show up with verifiable proof win the shortlist; firms without it get passed over for a competitor who has the same experience but displays it.

These fixes directly map to the issues above, but this section focuses only on action.

Section 05 · Action plan

Top 3 fixes

What To Fix First

  1. #1

    Add a machine-readable business identity to the homepage

    High ImpactDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix
    Install a structured business profile on the homepage that explicitly lists the firm name, phone numbers, office addresses, service specialty (executive search, commercial real estate), and years in operation — in a format AI tools can read directly without interpreting the page copy.
    Expected impact
    AI tools can now confirm and cite your firm when answering "who are the top CRE recruiters in Boston/New York/Dallas?" instead of defaulting to competitors.
  2. #2

    Create and publish a sitemap, and fix the robots.txt file

    Quick WinDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Quick win
    Fix
    Generate an XML sitemap covering all key pages (homepage, about, team, process, contact) and reference it from the robots.txt file so every AI citation tool and search engine has an explicit list of pages to read.
    Expected impact
    Your team bios, client quotes, and office locations become findable and citable — not hidden behind pages that AI tools may never reach.
  3. #3

    Add a visible, third-party-linked proof section to the homepage

    Trust SignalDifficulty: ModerateGeoViz Profile Setup: NoEstimated impact: Trust boost
    Fix
    Add a homepage section with at least a Google or LinkedIn review count, a named industry association membership (ULI, NAIOP, or similar that the firm already participates in per LinkedIn), and the "Founded 2001" credential displayed visually — not just buried in body copy.
    Expected impact
    A prospect or AI tool scanning the homepage for credibility signals finds verifiable proof within seconds, increasing both human confidence and AI citation likelihood.
