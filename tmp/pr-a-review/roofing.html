Section 04 · Diagnosis

Top 3 issues

What's Holding You Back

  1. #1

    Your business contact details don't match across your own site

    High Impact
    Problem
    The audit detected three conflicts in how the business name, phone number, or address appears across different parts of the site.
    Why it hurts visibility
    When AI tools see contradictory information, they lose confidence that they've found the right business and are likely to skip over it in favor of a competitor whose details are consistent.
  2. #2

    Your data blocks are missing the details customers actually ask about

    High Impact
    Problem
    The existing structured data doesn't include your business hours or a confirmed map location (geo-coordinates).
    Why it hurts visibility
    A customer asking "is this roofer open today?" or "where are they located?" gets no answer from your site — that question goes to whoever has it filled in.
  3. #3

    Your homepage barely has enough words to fill a sticky note

    High Impact
    Problem
    The homepage contains approximately 133 words, which is well below the 300-word minimum needed for AI tools to understand what you do and where you do it.
    Why it hurts visibility
    With so little content, there's almost nothing for an AI tool to quote or reference — meaning a competitor's more detailed page wins the recommendation instead.

These fixes directly map to the issues above, but this section focuses only on action.

Section 05 · Action plan

Top 3 fixes

What To Fix First

  1. #1

    Make your name, phone, and address match everywhere on the site

    High ImpactDifficulty: ModerateGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix
    Audit every page footer, contact section, and data block on the site and ensure the business name, phone number, and address are letter-for-letter identical across all of them.
    Expected impact
    AI systems gain a consistent, verifiable identity to work from — reducing the chance they skip or misrepresent the business.
  2. #2

    Add your hours and location to the existing data blocks

    High ImpactDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix
    Fill in the missing required fields in your site's existing data blocks: confirmed business hours, full street address, and map coordinates.
    Expected impact
    AI tools can now answer "are they open?" and "where are they?" — two of the most common questions before a customer calls.
  3. #3

    Expand each core service page to at least 300 words

    High ImpactDifficulty: ModerateGeoViz Profile Setup: NoEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix

    Expected impact
    Your pages become quotable — AI tools have real content to reference when a local homeowner asks who to call.
