Section 04 · Diagnosis

Top 3 issues

What's Holding You Back

  1. #1

    Your website isn't showing up anywhere online

    High Impact
    Problem
    The domain midwestdentalcare.com returned zero indexed pages — meaning Google and every AI tool that looked for it found nothing.
    Why it hurts visibility
    If your site isn't in the index, you won't show up when a patient nearby searches for a dentist, and AI tools have no content to pull from when recommending local providers.
  2. #2

    AI tools can't tell what your business does or where you are

    High Impact
    Problem
    There is no publicly visible content confirming your business name, the city you serve, the services you provide, or a phone number to call.
    Why it hurts visibility
    When a patient asks "who's a good dentist in [your city]?" and AI tools find no identity connected to your site, they pass you over and recommend a competitor who does have that information.
  3. #3

    You have no visible proof that patients trust you

    Trust Signal
    Problem
    No reviews, no ratings, no named dentist, and no credentials appear anywhere in public listings tied to this domain.
    Why it hurts visibility
    Patients making dental decisions want social proof before they call; without it, even a patient who lands on your site has no reason to book — and AI tools won't confidently cite a practice with no verifiable reputation.

These fixes directly map to the issues above, but this section focuses only on action.

Section 05 · Action plan

Top 3 fixes

What To Fix First

  1. #1

    Get your website into Google and AI search results

    Quick WinDifficulty: TechnicalGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Quick win
    Fix
    Create and submit a sitemap, ensure robots.txt is properly set up to allow all major search and AI tools to read your site, and confirm no technical blocks are preventing indexing.
    Expected impact
    Your pages start appearing in search results and become readable by AI tools for the first time.
  2. #2

    Add your business identity clearly to every page

    High ImpactDifficulty: ModerateGeoViz Profile Setup: YesEstimated impact: Strong gain
    Fix
    Add your practice name, city, phone number, address, and a plain-English description of your services to the homepage — plus set up the behind-the-scenes identity file (called a LocalBusiness tag) that tells AI tools exactly who you are, what you do, and where you work.
    Expected impact
    AI tools can now answer "who is a dentist near me?" with your name, location, and specialty instead of a competitor's.
  3. #3

    Collect and display visible proof of patient trust

    Trust SignalDifficulty: EasyGeoViz Profile Setup: NoEstimated impact: Trust boost
    Fix
    Ask satisfied patients to leave a Google review, then display your star rating and review count on your homepage; also add the name and credentials of your lead dentist to an About page.
    Expected impact
    Patients who land on your site have a reason to call, and AI tools have real-world proof to cite when recommending your practice.
